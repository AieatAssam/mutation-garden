# M1: Playable Garden

**Stopping point (2026-10-21):** Plan reset to five vertical slices (below). S1 garden look is in progress on `salvage/s1-look`. Next: finish S1, then S2 plants.

**Objective (brief):** Prove that planting, growing and harvesting are enjoyable.

## Scope (brief)

- One miniature garden scene.
- Six plots.
- Three plant species.
- Seed selection.
- Planting interaction.
- Animated growth.
- Harvesting.
- Basic coin rewards.
- Touch and mouse controls.
- Basic persistence.
- Simple sound and visual effects.

No mutation laboratory, multiplayer or advanced economy. (brief)

## Details from the rest of the brief

- **Plants (section 4):** Sunny Sprout 8 s, low value; Bubble Berry 15 s, medium; Twistroot 25 s, high. These are starting targets, kept in data tables. Stages: Seed, Sprout, Growing, Mature, Harvestable. Growth is animated smoothly, not by swapping models. Each species has its own silhouette and growth behaviour (Twistroot twists as it grows; Bubble Berry fruit is round and bouncy).
- **Growth rules (section 4):** plants grow on their own, mature plants wait until harvested, and closing the browser never loses a plant. Growth timers use real elapsed time (see docs/architecture.md, Time). Water once per plant moves into M1 (M1-18, owner request 2026-10-19); watering tools and upgrades stay in M3.
- **Camera (section 3):** three-quarter isometric-style view. Drag empty ground to move, pinch or wheel to zoom, within limits. No rotation needed for basic play. The camera never moves by accident while planting or harvesting.
- **Controls (section 8):** tap a seed in the bottom toolbar, tap an empty plot to plant, tap a mature plant or swipe across several to harvest. Mouse equivalents on desktop. Portrait first, landscape supported, safe areas respected, no page scrolling during play.
- **Feedback (sections 2, 10, 12):** seed drop with bounce, dust puff and sound; growth pop sound; obvious ripe signal; fruit detaches, bounces and flies to the collection area; coin chime; combo feedback when harvesting several plants. Separate sound and music controls; respect autoplay rules; reduced motion.
- **Pacing (sections 1, 7):** first planting within 10 s, first harvest within 30 s.
- **Look:** docs/art/style-guide.md (Seed Packet).

Not in M1: mutations (M2); shop, more plots, tools, watering upgrades, garden levels and offline-growth rules (M3); the lab (M4); sharing (M5).

## Implementation contracts

The brief wins conflicts. Section 13: "Gameplay systems must not depend directly on Three.js scene objects." Section 4: "Growth should be animated smoothly, not represented by sudden model replacement." Section 18: "Make the smallest possible garden irresistibly fun before making the garden bigger."

Use [architecture: M1 public contract](../architecture.md#m1-public-contract) and [ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-m1-simulation-and-adapter-contracts.md). The reducer takes **three** arguments: `step(state, commands, nowMs)`. RNG state lives in GardenState; there is no external RNG argument. Growth is timestamp-derived, and snapshots carry the presentation data needed by adapters.

## Salvage plan (2026-10-21)

**Why the plan changed.** After about 150 PRs, M1 had a correct sim, input and camera, but the garden on screen was six brown boxes on a green disc. Most effort went into splitting tasks, browser "proof" PRs and committed evidence PNGs. The throwaway prototype (`docs/art/prototype/shots/`) looked better than the game. From now on, every unit of work is a **vertical slice that ends with the game visibly better on a phone**. The old 47-task list is in git history at `df743ff`.

**Kept:** the sim (`src/sim`), fixed-step loop, lifecycle, pointer/swipe/pan/zoom input, picking, the material library, plot status (dial and stamp), the Seed Packet art direction and palette. These work and are tested.

**Changed:** visuals come from authored models, not hand-placed primitives. Models are made in Blender, exported as glTF to `public/models/`, and loaded with `GLTFLoader`. Each Blender material is named after a palette path (`environment.grass.1`, `plants.twistroot.fruit.1`), and the loader swaps it for the shared toon material from `materials.ts`, so palette.json stays the only colour source. Growth uses shape keys (morph targets) or bones driven by progress, never model swaps. See [ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-authored-models-and-slices.md).

Each slice is one PR, typically 400–1500 changed lines. Art review happens once per slice from the 390×844 and 1280×800 screenshots in the PR body. Do not commit per-task evidence PNGs.

### S1 — Garden look (in progress, branch `salvage/s1-look`)
Goal: the empty garden looks like a finished miniature diorama at phone size.
- Blender island: organic grass cap with a light lip, faceted rock strata from sandstone to mulberry, hanging roots, edge rocks and tufts ([liveliness 3](../art/m1-liveliness.md)).
- Props: wooden crate (harvest target), fence posts, watering can, small pond, flowers and grass tufts ([liveliness 5](../art/m1-liveliness.md)).
- Raised beds with rounded wooden or soil rims and furrows, authored in Blender.
- Portrait framing: the island fills the phone width; no empty half-screen.
- Done when: phone and desktop screenshots stand beside `proto-phone-day.png` and win.

### S2 — Plants that read
Goal: the three species look like the prototype or better, and grow smoothly.
- Blender models for Sunny Sprout, Bubble Berry and Twistroot following style guide section 5 silhouettes, with shape keys for growth (and twist for Twistroot).
- Replace the procedural rigs; keep the reconciler and plot-status contracts.
- Ripe signal: colour warm-up, wobble, halo ([liveliness 2](../art/m1-liveliness.md)).
- Done when: all three are recognisable at 48 px, growth has no pops except stage accents.

### S3 — Juice and sound
Goal: planting and harvesting feel good.
- Seed drop with squash, dust puff, growth pop (port `effects.ts` from `m1/12a1-render-accents`).
- Harvest: fruit stretches, detaches, arcs to the crate; coins fly to the counter; counter bumps.
- Swipe combo bonus in the sim (#112's rule, decided in the PR) and combo label.
- WebAudio sounds unlocked by first gesture: plant, pop, harvest, coin, combo. Sound and music toggles.
- Done when: a 30 s screen recording of plant-to-harvest feels like a finished mobile game.

### S4 — Saves and first minute
Goal: progress survives refresh and a new player knows what to do.
- Versioned IndexedDB save, save on visibility change, interrupted-write recovery, fixture-tested migration.
- Seed prices on the tray, one-line hints ("Tap a plot to plant.", "Swipe across ripe plants."), first harvest within 30 s.
- Done when: refresh keeps the garden; a new player plants within 10 s.

### S5 — Life and acceptance
Goal: the garden feels alive and M1 is accepted.
- Wind sway, paper clouds, butterflies, falling leaves; reduced motion respected.
- Performance pass on a mid phone (under 150 draw calls; adaptive pixel ratio).
- Milestone critique gate (AGENTS.md), then tick acceptance below.

After S5, M2 (mutations) starts with Golden and Crystal, which the prototype already shows.

## Open M1 weaknesses

- **Short landscape picker overlap:** 804×350 CSS-pixel canvases are outside 07b2b's accepted matrix; overlapping plot target squares can return a neighbouring ID near an edge/corner. The merged browser probes cover 844×390 full canvas and 828×374 with an 8 px inset, not the smaller canvas. Keep this as a render/camera follow-up before M1-16b mobile-interaction acceptance, preferably during 17a/17b when camera bounds are reviewed; do not claim all landscape sizes work or weaken the 48×48 target requirement. A fix needs its own focused task, regression probes and art review, and an ADR 0005 amendment if the accepted camera-envelope decision changes. It does not expand 08a or block the standalone Sunny rig.
