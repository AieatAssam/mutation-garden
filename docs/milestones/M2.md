# M2: Mutations and discovery

> Provisional. The brief's own Milestone 2 text is missing from docs/brief.md. This scope comes from brief sections 1 (pillar 3), 2 (Discover) and 17. Replace it with the brief's text when it arrives.

**Objective.** Make mutations exciting and visually meaningful, not just number multipliers.

## Scope

- The five mutation treatments in docs/art/style-guide.md section 3.4: Giant, Glowing, Golden, Crystal, Rainbow, with their materials, extras and mobile fallbacks.
- Deterministic mutation rules in `src/sim`: chances driven by the seeded RNG, plus simple conditions such as neighbouring species or watering. Exact rules are designed by the architect and recorded as an ADR.
- Mutation value multipliers in harvest coins.
- The first-time mutation reveal moment (style guide motion principle 5).
- A journal: species and mutations discovered, with silhouettes for undiscovered entries.
- Save schema migration for mutation and discovery data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each mutation is visually distinct from the normal plant and from the other mutations at phone size.
- [ ] Mutation rules are deterministic for a given save and command sequence (unit tests).
- [ ] A mutation is rolled once; reloading the save can't re-roll it.
- [ ] First-time discoveries trigger the reveal and add a journal entry; repeats don't trigger the slow-down.
- [ ] Mutated harvests pay the documented multiplier (unit tests).
- [ ] Crystal transmission stays within its on-screen cap, with the fallback used beyond it.
- [ ] Old M1 saves migrate cleanly (fixture test).
- [ ] Players can tell what caused a mutation (playtest note or journal hint).
- [ ] CI green with no retries on both Playwright projects.
