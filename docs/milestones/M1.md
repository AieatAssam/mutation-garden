# M1: Living garden toy

> Provisional. The brief's own Milestone 1 text is missing from docs/brief.md. This scope comes from brief sections 1, 2, 15, 17 and 18. Replace it with the brief's text when it arrives.

**Objective.** Make the smallest possible garden irresistibly fun: plant a seed, watch it grow, harvest it, and want to do it again. "A living toy first."

## Scope

- One floating island in the Seed Packet style (docs/art/style-guide.md), with 6 plots.
- The three starter plants: Sunny Sprout, Bubble Berry, Twistroot. Each has its own silhouette, 3–4 growth stages and a ripe signal.
- Plant by tapping a seed in the tray, then a plot. Harvest by tapping a ripe plant, or swiping across several.
- Coins from harvests, shown on the coin label. Seeds cost coins. The starting coins and a free first seed make sure the first planting is never blocked.
- Planting, growth, ripe and harvest animations and sounds, following the style guide's motion principles.
- Save and load to IndexedDB (schema v1), so a refresh keeps the garden.
- Fixed-timestep sim loop. Sim, render and input stay separate, as in docs/architecture.md.

Not in M1: mutations (M2), upgrades and new plots (M3), offline catch-up beyond a simple reload (M3), the lab (M4), sharing (M5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new player can plant within 10 seconds and harvest within 30 seconds, with no tutorial screen.
- [ ] Planting, growth and harvesting all work with one finger on a phone in portrait.
- [ ] Swipe harvesting collects several ripe plants in one gesture.
- [ ] The three starter plants are identifiable by silhouette alone.
- [ ] Every tap, growth stage and harvest has visible and audible feedback.
- [ ] The garden looks appealing with only one or two plants (art advisor sign-off against docs/art/style-guide.md).
- [ ] Refreshing the page restores the garden and coins exactly.
- [ ] Sim rules are deterministic and fully unit-tested; sim has no three, Rapier or DOM imports (lint).
- [ ] No raw hex colours outside `src/render/palette.ts` (lint and unit test).
- [ ] Playwright passes on `mobile-portrait` and `desktop`, CI green with no retries.
- [ ] Performance targets in docs/architecture.md are met or the gaps are written up with a plan.
