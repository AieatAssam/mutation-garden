# M2: Mutations

**Objective (brief):** Make experimentation and discovery compelling.

## Scope (brief)

- Five mutation traits.
- Explicit mutation rules.
- Visible mutation appearances.
- Discovery journal.
- First-discovery rewards.
- Controlled mutation opportunities.

## Details from the rest of the brief

- **Traits (section 5):** Giant (much larger; more value), Golden (metallic; higher rarity and value), Glowing (soft glow; unlocks special combinations), Crystal (crystalline leaves and fruit; valuable rare produce), Rainbow (animated multicolour; special collection reward). Visual recipes: docs/art/style-guide.md section 3.4.
- **Triggers (section 5):** cross-pollination between compatible neighbours, environmental conditions, specific fertilisers, rare natural events. (Laboratory combining is M4.) Players must be able to influence outcomes; randomness alone must not be the only path. The architect picks which triggers ship in M2 and records the rules in an ADR.
- **Discovery (section 5):** growth briefly pauses for a reveal, the plant gets its effect, a notification appears, the journal records it, and the player gets a meaningful first-discovery reward. The reveal is exciting but skippable.
- **Combinations (section 5):** later progression, such as Giant + Golden. Rules must be consistent, and the game must never hint at a discovery that is impossible to get.
- **Fairness (sections 7, 10):** progression must stay satisfying when rare mutations don't happen.
