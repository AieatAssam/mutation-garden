# M4: Mutation Laboratory

From the brief. Do not start any of this, or create files in `src/lab`, before M3 is accepted.

**Objective.** Introduce an additional tactile discovery mechanic.

## Scope

- Interactive laboratory scene.
- Physics-based specimen manipulation.
- Compatible specimen combinations.
- New seed discoveries.
- Merge effects and rewards.


Design: [../design.md](../design.md). Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md). Work in slices, one PR each ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-authored-models-and-slices.md)).

## Slices

### S1 — Lab scene and physics
- Lift the ESLint block on `src/lab` with an ADR. Glass jar scene beside the island; specimen tray; drag from tray and drop into the jar; Rapier bodies with pooling and caps.

### S2 — Recipes and chains
- Sim recipe table keyed on specimen kinds; fusion and chain reactions; invalid-pair feedback ([motion](../art/motion.md) 4.10).

### S3 — Lab species
- Prism Fig and Sunburst: models, seeds, journal entries.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Merging rules are deterministic.
- [ ] Physics does not affect reward correctness.
- [ ] Drag-and-drop works reliably on touchscreens.
- [ ] Invalid combinations produce understandable feedback.
- [ ] Laboratory activity does not corrupt garden state.

Implementation notes: merge results come from `src/sim` rules keyed on the specimens combined, never on physics outcomes. Rapier lives in `src/physics`. Lifting the ESLint block on `src/lab` is the first task of M4, with an ADR.
