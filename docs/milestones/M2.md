# M2: Mutations

**Stopping point:** M2 planned; next: S1 on m2/s1-mutations.

**Objective (brief):** Make experimentation and discovery compelling.

## Scope (brief)

- Five mutation traits.
- Explicit mutation rules.
- Visible mutation appearances.
- Discovery journal.
- First-discovery rewards.
- Controlled mutation opportunities.

Design: [../design.md](../design.md). Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md). Work in slices, one PR each ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-authored-models-and-slices.md)). Mutation outcomes and save rules: [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-mutation-engine.md).

## Slices

### S1 — Mutation engine and Giant

- Sim: schema 3, separate mutation RNG stream, per-plant `mutationSeed`, independent trait hashes, one Mature evaluation, and stored results (at most two, highest value first). Sprout tells are derived from current snapshot conditions.
- Saves: migrate schema 2; give legacy plants a fixed seed that yields no mutation; keep bad-luck counters, journal entries, first-discovery-paid flags and the scripted-Giant-used flag in state. Add `tests/fixtures/saves/schema-2.json` and its migration fixture test.
- Giant: water twice while growing (second water at Level 4), 3% natural chance, plus the scripted first Twistroot planted at Level 3 or above.
- Render and UI: Giant grows to 1.8× with two overshoot bounces; add the Sprout tell, the near-miss objective line ("2 of 3 conditions"), two-beat reveal with tier badge, full first-discovery reveal and short repeat reveal from [motion 4.9](../art/motion.md#49-mutation-reveal-first-discovery). The reveal is skippable. Register all M2 mutation materials in the `compileAsync` warm-up before ready.
- Tests: boot keeps its random seed for players; a test switch or seeded saves fix outcomes; `startWithSave` marks the scripted Giant as used. Keep random reveals out of ordinary E2E flows and give the reveal its own spec.
- **Done when:** schema-2 saves migrate without mutation rerolls, Giant and reveal behavior pass deterministic tests, and the skippable first/repeat reveals are visible in phone and desktop captures.

### S2 — Golden and Glowing

- Sim: Golden uses at least two orthogonal Sunny Sprout neighbours at any stage on the specified 2×3 plot grid; Glowing qualifies when growth overlaps a dusk window. Implement the rule odds, natural odds and qualifying-mature guarantees from [design section 6](../design.md#6-mutations-m2); compute dusk from timestamps so offline growth is exact.
- World and render: add the 240 s day cycle with its 60 s dusk window to the sim; cross-fade sky tint and light at dusk. Golden uses real metal with an environment map and gleam. Glowing uses an emissive material with a halo. Add foil shimmer to mutated fruit.
- Tests: cover neighbour edges, dusk-window boundaries and offline interval overlap with fixed outcomes.
- **Done when:** rule-based Golden and Glowing outcomes are reproducible, both traits read clearly on phone and desktop, and their deterministic tests pass.

### S3 — Crystal, Rainbow and combinations

- Sim: Crystal qualifies on pond-side plot-0 or plot-2 immediately after harvesting a Glowing fruit from that plot. A Golden, Glowing and Crystal harvest in one swipe sets Rainbow flags on those plots; the next seed planted on any flagged plot is Rainbow.
- Render: Crystal transmission stays within its on-screen cap and uses a fresnel fallback beyond it; add the Rainbow band shader and pair rendering for all ten trait pairs.
- Tests: cover the pond and Rainbow plot rules, stacked value and result ordering; draw-call specs stay below 150 with mutated plants present.
- **Done when:** Crystal, Rainbow and all trait pairs are reproducible and visually distinct, transmission fallback works at its cap, and draw-call specs stay below 150 with mutations present.

### S4 — Journal and first-discovery rewards

- Build the printed catalogue panel with species rows, trait columns and a pairs page. Undiscovered entries remain silhouettes; show hints only after a prerequisite is met.
- Pay first discovery once per entry: 10× mutated value in coins plus XP. Show honest odds and the "First on this device" stamp in the journal.
- Tick every M2 acceptance criterion after save, reward and journal checks pass.
- **Done when:** journal and reward progress persist across reloads, duplicate rewards cannot be paid, and all M2 acceptance criteria are ticked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Mutations are reproducible under defined conditions. (brief)
- [ ] Every mutation has a visible distinction. (brief)
- [ ] Players understand at least one way to influence mutation outcomes. (brief)
- [ ] Discovery progress persists correctly. (brief)
- [ ] No duplicate first-discovery rewards. (brief)
- [ ] Mutation rules are deterministic for a given save, inputs and times (unit tests); reloading can't re-roll a result.
- [ ] The reveal can be skipped, and growth resumes correctly after it.
- [ ] Crystal transmission stays within its on-screen cap, with the fallback beyond it.
- [ ] M1 saves migrate cleanly (fixture test).
- [ ] CI green with no retries on both Playwright projects.
