## Current engagement pass

The pass adds a persistent journal for four species and three traits: exact recipes, natural odds (Level 2 and charm rules), unknown entries, and earned “First on this device” stamps. Before maturity it shows live plot condition counts. Six cyclic orders never expire, skip locked crops, save progress (schema 5), and award coin/XP once. Discoveries bounce the journal button; orders use a chime, coin label, and notice. Hints offer a feasible plant, water, sell, or adjacent Golden action.

It builds on tactile planting and growth, harvest sound/effects, fruit physics and combos, plot expansion, safe controls, persistence, and offline growth. “Golden only on non-Sunny plants” was ruled out: greedy play reached first mutation at 6:27 and first purchase at 7:40. That change would hide a real player path; the Golden rule stays unchanged.

Weather and rotating-market timers are deferred; their fun is unproven. Share cards are M5, the lab M6, and multiplayer a non-goal. Retention, virality, and comprehension remain unproven pending playtests.
