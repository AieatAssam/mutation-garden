# M3: Progression

**Objective (brief):** Sustain interest beyond the first session.

## Scope (brief)

- Seed shop.
- Garden expansion.
- Basic upgrades.
- Garden levels.
- Objectives and achievements.
- Economy balancing.
- Offline growth.


Design: [../design.md](../design.md). Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md). Work in slices, one PR each ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-authored-models-and-slices.md)).

## Slices

- Research additions ([design](../design.md) 11): rotating seed stall (5-minute stock from a clock hash, rare seeds), surprise seed drops, a no-guilt "tended days" count.

### S1 — Shop and tools
- Shop panel (slide-up), seeds, watering can upgrades, sprinkler, golden trowel, harvest basket ([design](../design.md) 7.2).
- Purchases stamp "Paid" and fly to where they go; every tool changes something you can see.

### S2 — Garden expansion
- Levels 4–13 unlock table ([design](../design.md) 7.1). Plots 7–10 arrive as island chunks rising from the clouds; camera reframes automatically ([motion](../art/motion.md) 4.8).
- Blender: island chunks and the second tier, authored to slot onto the S1 island.

### S3 — Orders and new species
- Order board (1–3 slots) with coins, XP and seed-packet rewards.
- Puffcap, Lantern Pod and Star Melon: Blender models with growth shape keys and their hooks.

### S4 — Offline return and economy simulation
- "While you were away" label with one-tap harvest-all combo.
- Headless economy bot in CI asserting the pacing targets in [design](../design.md) 7.5; property tests for offline additivity.

### S5 — Decorations
- Gnome, windmill, bird bath, lanterns and bee hive on fixed decoration spots, each with one idle animation; bee hive has its pollination effect.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Players have meaningful upgrade choices. (brief)
- [ ] Progression does not stall unexpectedly. (brief)
- [ ] Economy values are validated through automated simulations. (brief)
- [ ] Offline growth cannot generate invalid or duplicated rewards. (brief)
- [ ] A 20-minute play session provides several meaningful milestones. (brief)
- [ ] Upgrades have a visible effect on the garden, not just numbers.
- [ ] Offline results are the same however the time away is split up, and reloading never adds rewards (property tests).
- [ ] CI green with no retries.
