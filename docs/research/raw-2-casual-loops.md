# Raw research 2: sticky casual loops (Haiku subagent, 2026-10-22)

Search results thin; items marked general are genre knowledge.

Ranked: waiting is part of the loop (Neko Atsume, Stardew: each return is a payout); near-miss / one more round (Suika; show progress to next mutation, "2 of 3 conditions"); hidden variants found over time (Neko rare cats); collection album with silhouettes; small rotating requests (Hay Day, Travel Town orders) refreshed on timestamps; chain reactions in merges (lab); simple rules with deceptive depth (Suika); level-ups that unlock something (Hay Day); soft streaks with forgiveness (Duolingo freezes, small effect); surprise drops (rare seed on harvest); gentle "while you were away" summary; prestige reset later; deliberate session ends (Cozy Grove "come back tomorrow"); cosmetic coin sinks (decor).
