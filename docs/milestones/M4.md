## Slices

### S1 — Watering and pollination objects
- Sprinkler once-per-plant watering; T2 waters twice and enables Giant. Bee visits neighbouring plants; T2 adds 10 percentage points to non-guaranteed rule chances for visited plants.
- **Done when:** object animation and sim outcomes agree, and repeated timestamps cannot water or grant duplicate bee visits.
- **Implemented:** Sprinkler tiers water each plant once, twice for Giant, and extend to adjacent plots at T3. Bee visits mark each growing plant once; Bee T2 adds 10 points to the Crystal rule chance. Authored one-mesh prop script and optional model loading are ready; runtime remains compatible with builds without `automation.glb`.
- **Status:** Simulation is implemented; acceptance is blocked on Blender export and review of prop animation alongside the effects.

### S2 — Replanting and harvest helper
- Seed hopper replants the last seed on harvested plots. Helper walks ripe plants to the crate; T2 increases speed, T3 carries two.
- **Done when:** no fruit or seed duplicates, full-crate behavior is respected, and each tier has a visible effect.
- **Implemented:** Successful plantings persist the last seed per plot. The hopper replants harvested plots, then fills prior empty plots by tier. The helper harvests on a timestamp threshold, speeds up per tier and carries two at T3; ordinary harvest enforces crate space and Comet Pear's two-item yield.
- **Status:** Simulation is implemented; acceptance is blocked on Blender export and review of visible helper and hopper behavior.

### S3 — Delivery cart and Auto-sell
- Add the final object and two upgrades; delivery consumes crate items through the ordinary stall sale rules.
- **Done when:** auto-sell cannot exceed stall capacity or bypass sale value, and the unlock is gated by Level 12 and 1,200 coins.
- **Implemented:** Delivery Cart starts the regular stall queue when crate stock arrives. It uses saved fruit values and the normal sale cadence; purchases require Level 12 and 1,200 coins. Cart tiers change route speed presentation.
- **Status:** Sale behavior is implemented; acceptance is blocked on Blender export and review of the cart object and route animation.

### S4 — Offline simulation and balance
- Run owned automation over timestamp intervals, cap each absence at 2 h, and summarize all work on return. Simulate economy targets from design section 13.
- **Done when:** split intervals equal one interval, reload cannot pay twice, automation rates progress toward 20/34/52 coins/min, and 2-hour cap is enforced.
- **Implemented:** Offline catch-up visits fixed one-second simulation boundaries, preserving automation across split intervals. Existing save-on-return and summary paths report watered plots, harvests, sales, and remaining crate contents. The 20/34/52 coins/min economy targets have not been simulated or tuned yet.
- **Status:** Offline behavior and summary are verified; economy acceptance is blocked pending rate simulation and tuning.
