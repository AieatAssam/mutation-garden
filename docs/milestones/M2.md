# M2: Mutations

**Objective (brief):** Make experimentation and discovery compelling.

## Scope (brief)

- Five mutation traits.
- Explicit mutation rules.
- Visible mutation appearances.
- Discovery journal.
- First-discovery rewards.
- Controlled mutation opportunities.


Design: [../design.md](../design.md). Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md). Work in slices, one PR each ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-authored-models-and-slices.md)).

## Slices

### S1 — Mutation engine and Giant
- Sim: mutation rolls at planting time from the seeded RNG, stored on the plant; learnable rules; bad-luck counters; stacking (max two) ([design](../design.md) 6).
- Giant: water twice. Scripted first Giant on the first Twistroot after Level 3.
- Render: Sprout-stage tell; full first-discovery reveal pipeline (pause presentation, shiver, charge, flash, wipe, label, card) and short repeat reveal ([motion](../art/motion.md) 4.9).
- Done when: rules are reproducible and reload-safe (unit tests); reveal is skippable; screenshots and a clip in the PR.

### S2 — Golden and Glowing
- Golden: "full sun" between two Sunny Sprouts; real metal per style guide 3.4, sparkles, gleam.
- Glowing: dusk phase (one day/dusk cross-fade cycle) or next to a Puffcap; emissive pulse and halo.
- Done when: a player can produce each on purpose within 5 minutes of reading its hint.

### S3 — Crystal, Rainbow and combinations
- Crystal: pond-side after a Glowing harvest; transmission with on-screen cap and fresnel fallback.
- Rainbow: Golden + Glowing + Crystal in one swipe makes the next seed on those plots Rainbow; scrolling band shader.
- Pair combinations render as one material plus the other's effect.

### S4 — Journal and first-discovery rewards
- Printed seed-catalogue journal: species rows, trait columns, silhouettes with hints only once a prerequisite is met; pairs page.
- First-discovery rewards paid once (unit tests); journal persists through saves (migration fixture).
- Done when: brief M2 acceptance is all ticked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Mutations are reproducible under defined conditions. (brief)
- [ ] Every mutation has a visible distinction. (brief)
- [ ] Players understand at least one way to influence mutation outcomes. (brief)
- [ ] Discovery progress persists correctly. (brief)
- [ ] No duplicate first-discovery rewards. (brief)
- [ ] Mutation rules are deterministic for a given save, inputs and times (unit tests); reloading can't re-roll a result.
- [ ] The reveal can be skipped, and growth resumes correctly after it.
- [ ] Crystal transmission stays within its on-screen cap, with the fallback beyond it.
- [ ] M1 saves migrate cleanly (fixture test).
- [ ] CI green with no retries on both Playwright projects.
