# M3: Economy loop

**Stopping point (2026-10-24):** M3-S1 crate and stall UI, sale effects, full-crate feedback, and screenshot preview are implemented. This session fixed the greedy balance harness, tuned the opening progression, updated its pacing coverage and refreshed the design table. Palette, typecheck, lint, unit tests, and build passed; Playwright was intentionally not run. Next: continue S2 selling upgrades.

**Objective:** Make harvesting feed a visible crate-to-stall sale loop and add the full eight-seed garden.

**Owner change (2026-10-23):** Brief sections 2 and 7 pay at harvest; this milestone replaces that with crate inventory and player-started stall sales. The brief's section 16 excludes dozens of species; this plan has eight.

## Scope

- Crate, market stall, queued selling and stall upgrades.
- Three new species, completing the eight-species roster.
- Seed shop and M2 plot progression integration.
- Keep mutation traits and automation out of this milestone.

Design: [../design.md](../design.md) sections 5 and 13. Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md) 4.12 and 4.14.

## Slices

### S1 — Crate and market stall
- Store fruit species, size and mutation traits in a 12-slot crate; full crate leaves the plant ripe and displays its full icon.
- Tapping the stall sells one fruit per 1.2 s; sales continue during gardening and calculate value at sale.
- **Done when:** harvest never creates coins directly, crate overflow loses nothing, and queued sale value matches displayed species, size and traits.

### S2 — Selling upgrades
- Add faster-counter tiers, 18/24-slot crates, a second counter and Fresh Produce sign at the levels and prices in design section 13.
- **Done when:** each purchase changes stall throughput, capacity or sale value; locked prices and levels are visible and invalid purchases are rejected.

### S3 — Three new seeds
- Add Clockvine, Dewbell and Comet Pear; authored Blender models, distinct silhouettes, stated growth/value/unlock values and behaviour hooks. Lantern Pod is part of the five-seed roster named by the owner; the earlier Star Melon proposal is cut to keep the total at eight.
- **Done when:** all eight species can be bought, planted, grown, harvested and sold; each new behaviour has deterministic coverage.

### S4 — Economy pacing and return summary
- Tune seed prices and sale cadence; show sold coins and crate contents in the return summary. Reconcile brief order-board goals with the new loop.
- **Done when:** simulated manual rates approach 12 coins/min; no fruit or sale is duplicated across reload or offline intervals; 2-hour offline cap is ready for M4.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Harvested fruit enters the crate and retains species, size and mutations.
- [ ] Full crate blocks collection without losing a ripe fruit.
- [ ] Stall sales continue while the player gardens and pay the sale-time value.
- [ ] Selling upgrades visibly change their named property.
- [ ] Eight species have unique silhouettes and one documented behaviour each.
- [ ] CI green with no retries on both browser projects.
