## Slices

### S1 — The earn, spend and discover loop

- Sim and save: stay on schema 3; migrate schema-2 saves with owned-shop and fertiliser fields. Buy validates price, level and ownership. Plot purchases add plot-6 and plot-7. Fertiliser is consumed by the next planting; Big can waters every growing plant once.
- Rules: two waters always give Giant from Level 1; two Sunny Sprout neighbours always give Golden; a neighbouring Puffcap always gives Glowing. Natural mutation chances begin at Level 2. No rule hint promises a chance when the rule guarantees the trait.
- Rewards: each trait multiplies harvest coins and XP. A species + trait pays its first-find bonus once: 10× its mutated base value in both coins and XP. Reveal and harvest labels show the amounts and multiplier.
- Shop: Plot 7 (40 coins, Level 2), Plot 8 (120 coins, Level 4), Big can (60 coins, Level 3), Fertiliser (25 coins each) and Lucky charm (150 coins, Level 5; +3 percentage points to natural odds). Locked and unaffordable items remain visible.
- Content and world: Puffcap unlocks at Level 4, costs 6, grows in 30 s and pays 14. Its Blender model has growth and harvest extras. The west chunk is a separate scene node and the two added plots stay hidden until bought.
- Tests: Buy rejection/effects, all three guaranteed rules, first-find once, plot expansion, schema migration and both browser projects buying Plot 7, planting there, watering twice, seeing Giant x2 and opening/closing Shop.
- **Done when:** `CI=1 npm run check` passes with zero retries and 390×844/1280×800 captures show the bought island and Shop.

### S2 — Crystal, Rainbow and combinations

- Sim: Crystal qualifies on pond-side plot-0 or plot-2 immediately after harvesting a Glowing fruit from that plot. A Golden, Glowing and Crystal harvest in one swipe sets Rainbow flags on those plots; the next seed planted on any flagged plot is Rainbow.
- Render: Crystal transmission stays within its on-screen cap and uses a fresnel fallback beyond it; add the Rainbow band shader and pair rendering for all ten trait pairs.
- Tests: cover the pond and Rainbow plot rules, stacked value and result ordering; draw-call specs stay below 150 with mutated plants present.
- **Done when:** Crystal, Rainbow and all trait pairs are reproducible and visually distinct, transmission fallback works at its cap, and draw-call specs stay below 150 with mutations present.

### S3 — Journal and first-discovery rewards

- Build the printed catalogue panel with species rows, trait columns and a pairs page. Undiscovered entries remain silhouettes; show hints only after a prerequisite is met.
- Pay first discovery once per entry: 10× mutated value in coins plus XP. Show honest odds and the "First on this device" stamp in the journal.
- Tick every M2 acceptance criterion after save, reward and journal checks pass.
- **Done when:** journal and reward progress persist across reloads, duplicate rewards cannot be paid, and all M2 acceptance criteria are ticked.
