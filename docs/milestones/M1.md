# M1: Playable Garden

**Objective (brief):** Prove that planting, growing and harvesting are enjoyable.

## Scope (brief)

- One miniature garden scene.
- Six plots.
- Three plant species.
- Seed selection.
- Planting interaction.
- Animated growth.
- Harvesting.
- Basic coin rewards.
- Touch and mouse controls.
- Basic persistence.
- Simple sound and visual effects.

No mutation laboratory, multiplayer or advanced economy. (brief)

## Details from the rest of the brief

- **Plants (section 4):** Sunny Sprout 8 s, low value; Bubble Berry 15 s, medium; Twistroot 25 s, high. These are starting targets, kept in data tables. Stages: Seed, Sprout, Growing, Mature, Harvestable. Growth is animated smoothly, not by swapping models. Each species has its own silhouette and growth behaviour (Twistroot twists as it grows; Bubble Berry fruit is round and bouncy).
- **Growth rules (section 4):** plants grow on their own, mature plants wait until harvested, and closing the browser never loses a plant. Growth timers use real elapsed time (see docs/architecture.md, Time). Watering is M3 (tools).
- **Camera (section 3):** three-quarter isometric-style view. Drag empty ground to move, pinch or wheel to zoom, within limits. No rotation needed for basic play. The camera never moves by accident while planting or harvesting.
- **Controls (section 8):** tap a seed in the bottom toolbar, tap an empty plot to plant, tap a mature plant or swipe across several to harvest. Mouse equivalents on desktop. Portrait first, landscape supported, safe areas respected, no page scrolling during play.
- **Feedback (sections 2, 10, 12):** seed drop with bounce, dust puff and sound; growth pop sound; obvious ripe signal; fruit detaches, bounces and flies to the collection area; coin chime; combo feedback when harvesting several plants. Separate sound and music controls; respect autoplay rules; reduced motion.
- **Pacing (sections 1, 7):** first planting within 10 s, first harvest within 30 s.
- **Look:** docs/art/style-guide.md (Seed Packet).

Not in M1: mutations (M2); shop, more plots, tools, watering, garden levels and offline-growth rules (M3); the lab (M4); sharing (M5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new player can plant without instructions. (brief)
- [ ] First harvest occurs within 30 seconds. (brief)
- [ ] All primary interactions work on mobile. (brief)
- [ ] No accidental double planting or harvesting. (brief)
- [ ] Progress survives refresh. (brief)
- [ ] Camera movement does not interfere with planting. (brief)
- [ ] Game remains responsive with all plots occupied. (brief)
- [ ] The three species are recognisable by silhouette and growth behaviour.
- [ ] Growth is animated smoothly, with no sudden model swaps.
- [ ] Sim rules are deterministic and unit-tested; gameplay code has no three, Rapier or DOM imports (lint).
- [ ] No raw hex colours outside `src/render/palette.ts` (lint and unit test).
- [ ] Playwright passes on `mobile-portrait` and `desktop`, CI green with no retries.
