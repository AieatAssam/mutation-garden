# M5: More mutations and polish

**Objective:** Complete the learnable mutation set and make the expanded garden readable, accessible and shareable.

**Owner change (2026-10-23):** Mutation traits Frozen, Electric and Twin move from the brief's M2-era five-trait scope into M5, after the economy and automation loops.

## Scope

- Add Frozen, Electric and Twin with guaranteed learnable rules, rare natural chances, distinct looks and journal hints.
- Keep Giant, Golden, Glowing, Crystal and Rainbow.
- Shareable mutation cards, photo mode, optional daily challenge, accessibility, performance and onboarding polish.
- Buyer line and occasional bonus prices at the stall.

Design: [../design.md](../design.md) sections 6, 9 and 13. Motion: [../art/motion.md](../art/motion.md) 4.14.

## Slices

### S1 — Frozen and Electric
- Add rules connected to Dewbell and Sprinkler, mutation looks, journal hints and reveals.
- **Done when:** each rule is reproducible and its trait reads without colour alone.

### S2 — Twin and mutation collection
- Add Clockvine neighbour rule, journal column and stacked trait presentation.
- **Done when:** all eight traits can be intentionally learned and persisted; no journal hint promises an impossible state.

### S3 — Sharing and return features
- Discovery cards, garden photo mode, date-hashed optional challenge, stall buyer line and bonus price orders.
- **Done when:** sharing has a download fallback, bonus prices are shown before sale, and no server/account is required.

### S4 — Accessibility and final polish
- Colour-blind cues, text scale, haptics setting, reduced motion, performance and onboarding pass.
- **Done when:** all M5 acceptance checks pass on mobile portrait and desktop and the milestone critique is resolved.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every mutation has a guaranteed one-sentence rule and rare natural chance where specified.
- [ ] Mutation cues remain distinct without colour.
- [ ] Screenshots can be exported on supported browsers.
- [ ] Core gameplay remains usable without an account.
- [ ] Reduced motion settings work and mobile performance meets project targets.
- [ ] New players understand the game without a lengthy tutorial.
- [ ] CI green with no retries and critique findings resolved.
